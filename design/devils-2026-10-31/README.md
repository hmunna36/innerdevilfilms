# Ten devils, 31 Oct 2026

The owner said the old devil was "not looking good" and, of a first redraw, "not yet right. give me 10 different
devils i'll choose". These ten were drawn by ChatGPT in the owner's Chrome, each in the same lit bedroom with the same
man (`reference_room.jpg`, rendered by the engine), so that only the devil changes. `ten_devils.jpg` is the sheet the
owner chose from.

1 Shadow Twin, 2 Gentleman, 3 Smoke Wraith, 4 Gaunt, 5 Goat, 6 Ember, 7 Parasite, 8 Classic, 9 Mask, 10 Many-Eyed.

**The owner picked 1, the Shadow Twin**: a dark, much taller copy of the man himself (his round head and stick body,
stretched thin), two short sharp horns, burning slanted eyes and a grin far too wide, full of white teeth; black with a
hot red edge and its own red light on the wall. The films draw it in code (`engine/cast.js`); these images are the
design reference only, never film frames.
