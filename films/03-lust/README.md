# Film 03: LUST | It Watches Back

- **YouTube:** https://youtu.be/aqiuimf3dCg. It was uploaded 27 Oct 2026 as private and goes public 28 Oct 2026 at 20:00 IST.
- **The thumbnail** (`thumb.jpg`) was drawn by ChatGPT from frames of the film.
- **What it is:** 1:50, 16:9, a devil-horror short with no narration.
  - An extreme ElevenLabs score carries it. The horror score turns into a training anthem at 6:00 AM.
  - The devil's words are on screen.
  - The story reads from the pictures alone.
- **Files here:** `SCREENPLAY.md` (the shot-by-shot timeline and the music) and `lust_phone.mp4` (a 720p copy).
- **Source** (to move into this repo with the new pipeline): `~/Desktop/lust-film/` on the Mac.
  - `lust.html` is the code-drawn film: every frame is `renderAt(t)` on a canvas.
  - `tools/` holds `el.mjs`, `score.mjs`, `gen.mjs`, `mix.py`, `mixlib.py`, `render.mjs` and `qc.sh`.
  - `audio/` holds the ElevenLabs music and sound effects.
