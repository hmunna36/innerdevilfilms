# LUST | It Watches Back — screenplay and music (fast cut, no narration)

**Logline:** Every night a kind whisper tells him it's fine. Every night the thing behind him gets a little closer.

- **Length and format:** 1:50 (110.5 s), 16:9, 1920×1080.
- **Pace:** 63 shots averaging 1.65 s. Every shot slowly pushes in, the devil creeps closer inside its shots, and the phone feed keeps scrolling. Cuts to the clock and to its face flash for two frames.
- **The devil:** properly visible every night and a little closer each time: the window, the foot of the bed, beside him, right behind him with its hands on his shoulders. It has a skull face, long horns, burning slit eyes, a crooked grin, spider arms, a red aura, black smoke and embers.
- **The phone:** when a notification slides in, the site moves down under it, so both stay visible. It is an original "XXX videos" site, not a real brand, with blurred video and an 18+ badge. Nothing explicit is drawn, even under the blur.
- **Times on screen:** no day counter. Late-night clocks for the nights; normal waking hours for the fight back (6:00 AM shower, 6:30 AM run).
- **Preview:** open `lust.html`; the words on screen are part of the film. The finished video is `LUST - It Watches Back.mp4`.

## No narration: music and words on screen

- **The music carries the film.** It is one extreme score, with a few heavy hits on top. Nobody speaks.
- **The devil's lines are words on screen.**
  - Kind lines are a soft whisper in pink italics, beside the phone or low in the frame.
  - Its real lines (VO11–VO14, VO21) slam in as big red, glitching capitals.
  - A line stays where it started even when the shot cuts.
  - The lines keep their VO numbers, which are their ids in the code.
- **It reads without the words too:**
  - The devil is bigger and closer every night.
  - From night 2 a trail of black smoke runs from his back into it: it lives in him and feeds on him. The trail snaps when he throws the phone.
  - The notifications carry icons (♥ Mom, ☎ missed calls, ✉ work, ♥ Maya).
  - His face shows every turn.
  - The call to Mom has connected rings, and Maya answers with a heart.
