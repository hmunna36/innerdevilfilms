# GAMBLING | The House Always Wins: the story (DRAFT, before the critic)

## Loglines
1. Every night he feeds a betting app the rent, until the thing dealing behind him shows its face.
2. Every night a kind voice tells him he is one win away, and every night it takes more: the rent, his sister's college
   fees, his mother's gold chain, until the screen fills with zeros and the house shows him who it is. (CHOSEN: the
   cruelest cost, the coldest line)
3. Every night he chases his losses into the dark, until the house deals the last card: him.

**Title:** GAMBLING | The House Always Wins. **Thumbnail hook:** IT ALWAYS WINS.
**Its device (new to this film):** the betting app HOUSE (an original logo: a gold house roof on a gold tile; a dark
green felt table, a BALANCE, cards, "BET" and "DOUBLE OR NOTHING" buttons, every loss stamped "HOUSE WINS" in red); the
devil DEALS: playing cards fly out of the dark from its claws and pile up on his floor night by night; his mother's gold
chain, taken by its claw; the black glass fills with zeros; he SNAPS his bank card in half; the comeback adds a new
place, a warehouse where he works a double shift; at the end a gold coin flips in the dark doorway.
**Costs (the banners):** Landlord (rent), Sis (her college fees, then "Dropped from college. It's okay."), Mom
(Grandma's gold chain).
**Length:** 1:52 (112 s), 16:9, 1920x1080, about 70 shots, average about 1.5 s, none over 2.6 s but the cold line,
the end card and the title.
