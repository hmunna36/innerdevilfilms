# GAMBLING | The House Always Wins: the story (LOCKED 28 Oct 2026, after two critic rounds)

## Loglines
1. Every night he feeds a betting app the rent, until the thing dealing behind him shows its face.
2. Every night a kind voice tells him he is one win away, and every night it takes more: his sister's college fees, the
   rent, his mother's gold chain, until the screen fills with zeros and the house shows him who it is. (CHOSEN: the
   cruelest cost, the coldest line)
3. Every night he chases his losses into the dark, until the house deals the last card: him.

**Title:** GAMBLING | The House Always Wins. **Thumbnail hook:** IT ALWAYS WINS.
**Its device (new to this film):** the betting app HOUSE (an invented logo: a roof with one red eye; dark green felt, a
BALANCE, cards, "BET" and "DOUBLE OR NOTHING" buttons, every loss stamped "HOUSE WINS" in red, the one win a sour dead
green). The devil DEALS: cards fly out of the dark from its claws and pile up on his floor night by night; its claw takes
his mother's gold chain; DOUBLE OR NOTHING takes everything; the black glass fills with zeros; it deals him one last card
with his own face on it; he SNAPS his bank card in half and every card burns. The comeback adds a new place, a warehouse
where he works a double shift. Weeks later a gold coin (the HOUSE roof on one face, its grin on the other) spins in the
dark doorway.
**Costs (the banners, each one alone on the phone for at least 1.6 s):** Sis (her college fees), the Landlord, Mom
(Grandma's gold chain), Sis again ("I had to drop out. It's okay ♥").
**Length:** 1:57 (117 s), 16:9, 1920x1080, 75 shots, average about 1.5 s, none over 2.6 s but the end card (3.5 s) and
the title (3.9 s).
