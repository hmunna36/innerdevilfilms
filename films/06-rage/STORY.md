## Loglines
1. Every time he snaps at home, the shadow on the kitchen wall gets bigger, until the cracked mirror shows him whose
   hands those are.
2. One night in charge of his little brother, every time he loses it, the thing that holds his fists grows: a pot, a
   shout, a hole in the wall, one blow in the hallway, until his brother's own small shadow starts growing horns. (CHOSEN: the
   cruelest cost is not what he breaks, it is what his brother is learning; and the coldest line)
3. Every time he loses it, it gets a little taller, and his little brother gets a little smaller.

**Title:** RAGE | It Holds His Fists (25 characters; no exact match in the demand check; the cold line is not repeated in it). **Thumbnail hook:** IT GROWS.
**The cold line:** "EVERY TIME YOU LOSE IT… IT GROWS.", typed over his trembling fist. Its callback at the end: "EVERY TIME YOU DON'T…" "IT STARVES.", right after its last line, "LOSE IT ONCE. THAT'S ALL I NEED."
**The people he is losing:** SAM, his little brother (about 8; the same drawing style as him, smaller, with a big round
head; his crayon drawing on the fridge: two stick figures holding hands, "ME + MY BROTHER"), and MOM, on a night shift,
in banners (she left him in charge; Sam texts her from his room).
**Safety:** he never touches Sam and never gets to him: he breaks things (a pot, the kitchen wall, one blow on the hallway wall beside the mirror; Sam's door is never touched); the door holds; Sam never raises his hands or covers his head (he freezes, his crayon snaps; later he watches the door from his bed with his blanket pulled up), the devil only ever stands behind HIM, never over Sam, and its whisper at the door is a kind lie ("You just want to talk."), never a push at the child; Sam is the one who opens the door, and that night he tells Mom the truth: "No. I scared him. I'm getting help." No blood, no injury, nothing graphic; the
cost is the flinch. The comeback is real training and a real anger skill (breathing, opening his hands, walking away from it,
patching the wall with Sam), and he says sorry.
