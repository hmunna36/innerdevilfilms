# THE BOTTLE | It Was So Small: the cut (1:50.5, 30 fps, 1920 x 1080)

Times are in `cues.js` (one table for picture and sound). No narration. The story is in `STORY.md`.
The look: a black stage, cream cartoon people, a black devil with a red glowing edge, handwritten words.
The devil is **likeable** until AGE 38: small, funny, polite. Each time it is fed, the fire in its belly flares
and it pops a size bigger. Then he starves it and it shrinks back, size by size.
