# THE BOTTLE | It Was So Small (story v3, 17 Nov 2026)

Replaces the bar story (v2, "She Waited Till Dark"). About 1:45. No narration; the devil's words on screen.
Structure: THE YEARS (not used by films 03-06). Proposed, not built: it waits for the owner's choice of look.

## In one line

A man feeds something small for seventeen years. It grows every time, until it sits in his chair and he is the
small one. Then he starves it back down to the size it was when they met.

## The devil (the star)

- **It never lies.** Every line is true when it says it. That is why it works.
- **It never takes.** Everything is handed to it.
- **It adores him.** He is the one who feeds it. It is polite, grateful, funny, the best friend he has.
- **It does not want his soul. It wants his place.** It copies him a little more each time: his laugh at 21, a bow tie
  like his at 26, his shirt and tie at 34, his chair and his wedding ring at 38.
- **One rule the audience learns without a word:** each time it is fed, the fire in its belly flares and it grows a
  size. Its shadow is always one size ahead of it.
- When it is starved it runs the same sizes backwards, and its manner with them: giant and commanding, his size and
  mocking, child-sized and sulking, cat-sized and begging, thumb-sized and furious.

## The people

**Sam** (Dad): 21 and delighted in the first scene, 38 and worn out at the birthday. **Lily**, his daughter: a yellow
blanket, then a yellow dress; hers is the only yellow in the film. Her crayon drawings tell the truth twice.

## Why this is better than v2

- One idea a viewer gets with the sound off in five seconds: **it grows when he feeds it.** The channel's name on screen.
- The devil has a part to play at every size, and the audience likes it before they fear it.
- A real twist in pictures: the same birthday seen twice, with and without the devil.
- The child's drawing proves the damage, and later the repair, without a word.
- The ending is the opening, reversed: the same small thing, behind glass, still polite.

## Safety

Drinking is shown as harm, never glamorised; no brands or labels; he is 21 at his first drink; nothing graphic; the
comeback is real training at waking hours. The owner's rule "only the Shadow Twin" is replaced by the new devil only
if the owner approves a look.
