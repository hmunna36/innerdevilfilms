# Film 07: THE BOTTLE | She Waited Till Dark

Made on the Mac, 16 Nov 2026, at the owner's request ("create a new video below 2 minutes… I still couldn't get it
right"). Target 1:44 (104.2 s), 1920x1080, 30 fps, no narration.

## What is different in this film (and why)

Films 03-06 were watched 5 to 14 times each. Looking at them cold: small figures in wide, flat rooms; the cost told by
small text on phone screens; full-screen clock cards; the same gym montage at the end; nobody to care about. This film
changes the craft, not the channel:

1. **One person to care about.** Lily, six, in a yellow raincoat, waiting on the school steps. Her yellow is the only
   yellow in the film. The cost is one picture that gets worse three times, with no text at all.
2. **One device, visible in every part.** Everything he gives up goes into a bottle on the devil's shelf: his watch
   (the time), his ringing phone (the people calling), her photo (her). He slides each one across himself.
3. **Big, simple pictures.** Close shots, strong light and depth (lamp cones, haze, rain, reflections, blur in front
   of the lens), a camera that moves for a reason. Every shot must read on a phone at arm's length.
4. **The comeback is for someone.** The training is real (push-ups, a run, pull-ups, at 6 AM), and she is in every
   shot of it: counting on his back, on her bike ahead of him, hanging from the next bar.
5. **Text only where a picture cannot say it.** The devil's lines, three clocks that are part of the set, two phone
   screens. No clock cards.

## Structure, place, palette (rule 13: none used by films 04-06)

- **Structure: THE DEAL.** The devil is the bartender. Round after round it pours "on the house" and he slides
  something across the bar. Its shelf fills; it comes closer each round: behind the bar, leaning over it, on the stool
  beside him with its arm round his shoulders.
- **Main place:** a bar at dusk (new set), cut against the school steps (new set). Comeback: his kitchen at sunrise, a
  river bridge, a playground.
- **Palette:** deep teal shadows and amber glass light in the bar (04 was casino green and gold, 05 icy blue, 06 ember
  orange); cold indigo rain outside; Lily's yellow; the devil's red; pink-gold dawn for the comeback.
- **Reveal device:** the bottle with her inside, set on the bar in front of him.
- **Thumbnail composition (not used before):** the object of the vice huge in the foreground with someone trapped
  inside it: one bottle, the tiny girl in yellow inside with her hands on the glass, the devil's grin warped through
  the glass behind it. Word THE BOTTLE, hook "IT KEEPS HER".

## Spine and last line (written first)

A father wanted one drink before picking up his daughter, but every drink cost him something he slid across the bar
himself, so by the time the bottle was empty she was the thing inside it.

- Cold line: **YOU DRINK TO FORGET… / GUESS WHO REMEMBERS.**
- The devil's last line: **SHE WAITED. SO CAN I.**
- The callback and its answer (the last words of the film): **GUESS WHO REMEMBERS. / SHE DOES.**

## The critic (one round, 16 Nov 2026) and what changed

An independent reader scored version 1: cold open 7, stake by 0:15 5, the devil closing in 8, the cost 7, the reveal 5,
the comeback 5, the ending 6, clarity 5, ad safety 7, pace 6. Taken: (1) the hug is withheld on the night (she stops a
step short, walks home ahead of him) and the jump on DAY 90 is the film's first embrace; her trust returns in steps
through the comeback (the doorway, the bike, the next bar, his back, her hand); (2) the third bottle holds a plain
photo until the twist, when she turns and knocks, cut against the real Lily's knuckles on the step; (3) the symbol and
the real child stay apart: the smash frees his phone, still ringing SCHOOL, and the photo; nothing enters Lily; (4) the
teacher never leaves her and is the one calling; the night ends at 7:45, not 8:40; (5) the walk and lock screen became
one close shot, and the real Lily appears at 8.6; (6) round 3 cut to 5.6 s, the run to 3.5 s; (7) no second shriek at
the bar: the glass stays and the rain fills it; (8) the callback plays over the last picture, not on black; (9) plainer
lines for viewers whose first language is not English ("First one's free.", "YOU HANDED HER OVER.", "YOU'RE NEXT.").
Not taken: "I'LL WAIT. I ALWAYS DO." (LUST already ends on "I'LL WAIT."); the last line is "SHE WAITED. SO CAN I.".

## Safety

Drinking is shown only as harm: nothing looks fun, no brand, no label art beyond a plain shape, an invented bar with a
plain "BAR" sign. The girl in the bottle is a symbol (the photo he gave away); the real child is never touched,
threatened or hurt by anything: she waits, cold and sad, and is found. No gore, no blood, no self-harm. The devil never
goes near her.
