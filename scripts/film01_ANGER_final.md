**How this was made:** Claude's draft v1 was argued out with ChatGPT and the two converged on this version. Chat: https://chatgpt.com/c/6ad55ae0-d794-83ec-875a-fcac96878677

**Length:** about 3:00. Wordless. All dialogue is on-screen text: the narrator in white at the bottom, the Angel in gold, the Devil in red, Bean and the Friend in plain white beside them.

**The spine:** Bean practises one sentence 40 times to be heard. Then he uses his voice to silence the one person who tried to help him.

**The counter (top-left, unlabelled, never resets):**
- Act 1: each tick is one practice repetition, from 0 up to 40.
- Act 2 onward: each tick is Bean choosing to replay the failure. It keeps rising: 41, 87, 300.
- Same number, new meaning. The Devil doesn't stop his persistence; it changes its direction.

**THE image (thumbnail and screenshot):** Bean, lit by the dark, smiles after hurting her. The Devil behind him wears the exact same smile. Bean's smile fades. The Devil's doesn't.
