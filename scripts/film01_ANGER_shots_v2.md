# ANGER: animated shot list v2 (about 2:30)
The same story as `film01_ANGER_final.md`, re-cut for motion. Every shot is 2–5 s and something happens in it. Nobody just stands.

## Hook (0:00–0:04)
1. Tight on Bean on stage, notes in his hands, mouth opening. A single laugh off-screen. Hard cut to black. White text: "He practised this sentence forty times."

## Act 1: the Angel (0:04–0:48)
2. **Bedroom, night.** Bean is curled up, his face lit by the phone. He blinks slowly and drops the phone onto his chest. Narrator: "He had stopped expecting much from himself."
3. A gold spark drifts in through the window and loops around his head. He sits up, blinking, and follows it with his eyes.
4. The spark blooms into the Angel, who bobs and points toward the bathroom. Bean gets up and walks there (a walk cycle).
5. **Mirror.** He lifts his notes and tries the sentence; his mouth moves, then he stalls and rubs his face. ANGEL: "Again." Counter: 1.
6. **Montage, a 2 s beat each, counter ticking with every attempt (2 → 39):**
   - at the mirror again
   - on his bed, mouthing it
   - walking down the corridor, practising
   - in the mirror, his shoulders a little higher each time
   - The Angel cheers small: a spin, a clap.
7. Attempt 40: he gets through it. He freezes, then a tiny fist pump. The Angel does a happy loop. ANGEL: "You did it."

## Act 2: the humiliation (0:48–1:18)
8. **Presentation room.** He walks up to the lectern, and the spotlight comes on. The Friend in the front row gives him a small wave and a thumbs-up.
9. He starts; his mouth moves. His voice catches and his body jolts.
10. ONE laugh. Camera snap-zoom onto his face. The sound cuts to a ringing silence. The Angel's glow flickers.
11. He tries again. Nothing comes out. He lowers his notes slowly and walks off the stage.
12. **Corridor.** The Friend hurries after him and touches his arm. FRIEND: "I couldn't hear you at the end." He pulls his arm away, turns and walks off fast.

## Act 3: the Devil (1:18–2:18)
13. **Bedroom, night.** Bean is on his bed. The stage flashes back three times (the laugh each time); he flinches at every flash. Counter: 41, 42, 43. Narrator: "He practised it again."
14. Black smoke seeps in under the door, crawls across the floor and circles the bed. Red eyes open in it.
15. The Devil rises behind him and leans in close. DEVIL: "You heard one laugh." … "Now that's all you'll hear." The counter climbs fast.
16. The Devil's hand settles on his shoulder. Bean's fists clench and his brows drop. DEVIL: "Don't let them do it again." His eyes flash red for a moment.
17. **Corridor, next day.** The Friend walks up, smiling, and holds out her notes. FRIEND: "We could practise together."
18. The Devil's arms wrap around Bean from behind like a puppeteer's. DEVIL: "Make her feel it."
19. Bean steps forward and points. BEAN: "I don't need your pity. You couldn't even help me."
20. Her smile drops. She lowers the notes, turns and walks away slowly.
21. Bean smiles. Cut to the Devil behind him wearing exactly that smile. Bean's smile fades; the Devil's doesn't. Narrator: "For one moment, he wasn't the one who felt small."

## Ending (2:18–2:32)
22. The Devil swells to fill the room, and the room goes red. Bean sits small in the middle. The Angel is a tiny ember in the corner. The counter races to 300. Narrator: "He couldn't bear being laughed at."
23. Near-black. Bean is still, with the Devil behind him. Narrator: "So he learned to make others afraid to laugh." Cut to black. ANGER.
