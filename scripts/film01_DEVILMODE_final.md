# Film 01: DEVIL MODE (final shot list, about 2:30, fully animated)
**Harshad's idea:** when the Angel's motivation fails, Devil mode helps him achieve it.

**How we got here:** Claude's v3 was argued with ChatGPT.
- **Taken from ChatGPT:**
  - The Angel genuinely helps, but lets him skip the one hard sentence (a seeded failure).
  - Devil mode works but costs him.
  - At the climax he stumbles and recovers in public.
  - No revenge stare at the person who laughed.
- **Overruled:** ChatGPT wanted Bean to reject the Devil at the end. Harshad's idea is that Devil mode gets him there, so the Devil keeps its credit.

**The counter is the spine.** Gentle attempts stall at 40; Devil-mode reps race to 300.

**Closing line:** "Kindness got him started. The Devil got him there."
