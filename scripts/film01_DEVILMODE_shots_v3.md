# Film 01: DEVIL MODE, animated shot list v3 (about 2:30)
**Harshad's idea:** when the Angel's motivation fails, Devil mode is what helps him achieve it.
**Working title:** DEVIL MODE — "Kindness Got Him Started. The Devil Got Him There."
**Same cast:** Bean, the Angel, the Devil, the Friend.

**The counter:** it is the spine.
- Act 1 counts gentle attempts and stalls at 40.
- Act 3 counts Devil-mode repetitions, all the way to 300.

## Hook (0:00–0:04)
1. Stage. Bean's mouth opens. One laugh. Black. Text: "Kindness got him to forty."

## Act 1: the Angel tries (0:04–0:45)
2. **Bedroom.** Bean is curled up with his phone. A gold spark loops in and becomes the Angel.
3. The Angel coaxes him up. Bean walks to the mirror and tries the sentence. ANGEL: "That's okay. Try again."
4. **Montage of soft attempts.** He's half-hearted: he stops halfway, checks his phone, and the Angel cheers anyway. Counter 1 → 40.
   - ANGEL: "Good job!", "Rest now.", "You're doing great."
   - (We feel it's too soft.)

## Act 2: it fails (0:45–1:10)
5. **Presentation room.** The Friend waves. He starts. His voice catches. One laugh. Snap-zoom, then silence.
6. **Corridor.** FRIEND: "I couldn't hear you at the end." He walks off.
7. **Bedroom.** The Angel floats in. ANGEL: "It's okay. There's always next time." Bean turns away from it. The Angel's glow dims. The counter freezes at 40.

## Act 3: Devil mode (1:10–2:10)
8. Smoke under the door. The Devil rises, grinning. DEVIL: "Kind didn't work." … "Let's do it my way."
9. **Devil mode begins.** The room turns red. The clock reads 5:00 AM. The Devil throws the phone in a drawer. DEVIL: "Again." (The same word as the Angel's "Again" in the original, now hard.)
10. **The montage**, fast cuts on the beat, the counter racing 41 → 300:
    - at the mirror, over and over
    - pacing the room reciting
    - in the corridor before anyone arrives
    - in the empty presentation room, alone on stage
    - The Devil prowls, snapping its fingers. Every time he stalls, it replays the laugh, and he restarts harder.
11. Bean's eyes harden. His voice gets steady. The Devil's grin widens.

## The return (2:10–2:25)
12. **Presentation room again.** The same lectern, the same spotlight. The same person who laughed is there.
13. He speaks: every word lands. Snap-zoom on the laugher, silent now. The Friend's eyes go wide, and she smiles.
14. Silence, then applause.

## Ending (2:25–2:35)
15. Bean walks off stage. In the corner, the Angel's ember flickers back on. Behind him, the Devil smiles. He doesn't look back at either.
16. Text: "Some days you need the angel." … "Some days you need the devil." Title: DEVIL MODE.
