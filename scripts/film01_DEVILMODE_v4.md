# DEVIL MODE v4: the stakes + the twist (draft, about 2:30)
**Combines:** option 2 (he is about to lose everything) and option 1 (the Devil is the Angel in horns).

**The counter is the spine.**
- It counts down DAYS LEFT to rent: 30 → 0.
- In Devil mode it flips to count UP what he earns: ₹0 → ₹48,000, the rent plus his mother's medicine.

## Hook (0:00–0:05)
1. A notice slides under the door: "RENT DUE — 30 DAYS OR VACATE." Bean picks it up. A phone buzzes beside it: MOM.

## Act 1: the Angel comforts (0:05–0:50)
2. MOM, as a text on screen: "Don't worry about me. The medicine can wait."
3. The Angel appears. ANGEL: "You're doing your best."
4. **Soft montage**, DAYS LEFT 30 → 9:
   - He sends one job application, then the Angel says "Rest now".
   - He sleeps in.
   - A rejection email arrives. ANGEL: "Their loss."
   - He scrolls in bed. ANGEL: "Tomorrow."
   - A second notice: "FINAL WARNING."
5. Each comfort is kind, and each one costs him a day.

## Act 2: the floor (0:50–1:10)
6. Day 9. MOM, a text: "They stopped my medicine. I'm fine."
7. He sits on the floor, surrounded by notices. ANGEL: "It's okay. You tried." He looks at the Angel, and for the first time he's angry at it. The Angel's glow dims.

## Act 3: Devil mode (1:10–2:05)
8. Smoke under the door. The Devil rises. DEVIL: "Okay is how you lose her."
9. It's 4:00 AM, the room is red, and the phone goes in the drawer. DEVIL: "Again."
10. **Montage**, the counter flipping to ₹ and climbing:
    - at the laptop, typing
    - delivering parcels in the rain
    - fixing a stranger's phone at a stall
    - rejected, then straight to the next one
    - the Devil prowls; when he slows, it shows him Mom's text again
11. The cost: the Friend texts "Coming tonight?" He doesn't reply. Her empty chair at a table.
12. Day 0. He slides the money under the landlord's door and sends Mom: "Medicine paid." MOM: "Thank you, beta."

## The twist (2:05–2:25)
13. He sits alone, exhausted, with the Devil behind him. He finally turns around to face it.
14. The Devil steps into the light. The smoke peels away, the horns flicker out, and it's the Angel, quiet and tired.
15. ANGEL: "You never listened when I was kind."
16. Bean stares. The Angel's halo glows back on, faintly.

## Ending (2:25–2:32)
17. Card: "Same voice." … "You just needed it louder." Title: DEVIL MODE.
