# Film 01 — draft v1 (Claude, 19 Oct 2026)

**Working title:** ANGER — "He Was Getting Better. Then They Laughed."
**Length:** about 3:00. Wordless; on-screen lines only (narrator white at the bottom, the Angel in warm gold, the Devil in red).
**Cast:** Bean; the Angel (warm gold, a halo, kind); the Devil (black smoke, red rim, horns). Both voices are parts of him.
**Counter:** top-left, "DAYS BETTER".

## Cold open (0:00–0:06)
On black: "He was finally getting better." / then bold: "Then one laugh undid all of it."

## Act 1: the Angel arc, the climb (0:06–1:10)
1. **Lost** (DAY 0). Bean curled on his bed at 2 AM, phone light on his face. The narrator: "He didn't hate himself. He just stopped expecting anything."
2. **A small whisper.** A tiny gold glow by the window. The Angel: "one push-up. that's all." He does one, and collapses laughing at himself. DAY 1.
3. **Listens.** A quick montage, cut every 1.5 s: alarm, a run in the rain, books, a notebook with ticks. The Angel cheers small wins. The counter climbs: 7, 21, 40.
4. **Builds habits.** At a desk, practising a presentation to an empty room. He mouths the words, terrified. The Angel: "you've practised this 40 times."
5. **The setup.** A poster: "OPEN MIC NIGHT / TALENT SHOW — FRIDAY". He signs up. DAY 60.

## Act 2: the humiliation (1:10–1:45)
6. **Friday.** A stage, faceless silhouettes in the crowd. He freezes and his first word cracks. Silence, then one laugh, then everyone. A phone is held up, recording.
7. Played in near-silence: the sound bed drops out; only the laugh is left, slowed down. The Angel's glow flickers. The narrator: "It lasted eleven seconds."
8. **Home.** The clip has 40k views. The comments scroll ("bro froze lmao"). The Angel: "it's one night. you're still—" and the Devil's smoke covers the Angel's line mid-word.

## Act 3: the Devil arc, the fall (1:45–2:40)
9. **First whisper.** Red eyes open in the dark corner. The Devil: "they'll never forget it." It quotes the Angel's own words back, twisted: "one push-up? you can't even say one word."
10. **Influence.** He deletes the presentation file. He skips the run. The counter flickers.
11. **Partnership.** A hand of smoke on his shoulder. The Devil: "you don't need them. you need them to pay." He starts typing an angry reply under the video. Typing, deleting, typing again.
12. **Control.** His eyes glow red. He sends it. Then another, and another. The counter breaks: DAYS BETTER ▸ 0. The Angel is a tiny ember, almost out.
13. **The test.** 2 AM again, the same bed as scene 1, but now he isn't empty, he's burning. The Devil sits where the Angel sat. It smiles: "see? you were never the one getting better."

## Ending (2:40–3:00)
14. On black: "Anger doesn't arrive." / bold: "It moves into the room you left empty."
15. Title card: "ANGER" · PART 1, and a tagline: "The Angel is still there. He just stopped listening."
