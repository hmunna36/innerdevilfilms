# Short 06: tomorrow-cost (29.8 s, 1080 x 1920)

What Devil Mode cost him in film 01 TOMORROW, held in near silence. It opens on his red eyes at the laptop with
"$2,400 in 18 days." and the question "What did it cost him?". Day 1: his phone goes into the drawer. The nights: 9:31,
12:40 at the keys ("No nights off."), 2:40, he rubs his eyes and the Devil's hand lands on him ("No sleep."), 6:02 and
still up, swaying beside the grinning Devil. Friday 7:45: Ava's "Dinner tonight? I saved you a seat." lights up in the
dark drawer; his eyes flick to it and he keeps typing. The restaurant table with its candle and the empty chair
("He never came."). The huge Devil over him. Day zero: PAID $1,800 Rent, PAID $600 Mom's medicine, MISSED Dinner with
Ava. Mom's "Got my heart medicine today. Thank you, sweetheart." in the drawer. He closes the lid, eyes shut, the Devil
still beside him: "Worth it?". Then the end card ("He kept saying tomorrow." / TOMORROW / Full film on the channel).
