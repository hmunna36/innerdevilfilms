# Short 07: tomorrow-reveal (27.0 s, 1080 x 1920)

The reveal of film 01 TOMORROW: the voice was always the same. It opens on the Devil still beside him at 9:04, rent
and medicine paid, with "The Devil saved him." and "Then he saw who it really was." He turns and faces it; it reaches
for him. Red turns gold and the horns melt into a halo; a beat of silence; the flash: it is the Angel. She reaches for
him, and on the wall the shadow wears a halo now. His tear. "You never listened when I was kind." The RENT and
MEDICINE papers on the floor, both PAID. "I heard you. I kept choosing tomorrow." The film's last lines over the last
of the shot ("The voice was always the same." / "The choice was finally his."), then the end card ("He kept saying
tomorrow." / TOMORROW / Full film on the channel).
